About Our School

A landmark of learning in Kharian Cantt

FG Public School (Boys), Kharian Cantt is part of the Federal Government Educational Institutions (FGEI) network serving cantonments and garrisons across Pakistan. Our two-storey main building — with the school name proudly written across its facade — opens onto a green, tree-lined campus with open grounds for assembly, sport and national-day celebrations.

  • Boys' education from junior classes through Matric (SSC)
  • Experienced, dedicated teaching staff under FGEI
  • Green campus with large playground and assembly ground
  • Equal focus on studies, sports and character building

Academics

Learning at every stage

A clear path from the first classroom to the Matric examination — with close attention at each stage.

📚
Junior Section

Primary Classes

Strong foundations in Urdu, English, Mathematics, Science and Islamiat — reading, writing and good habits built from day one, with activity-based learning for young boys.

✏️
Middle Section

Classes VI – VIII

Deeper study of English, Mathematics, General Science, Social Studies and Computer Science, with regular tests, homework checks and class competitions.

🎓
Secondary Section

Matric — IX & X

Focused board-examination preparation in Science and Humanities groups — Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Computer Science and Mathematics — with revision tests and pre-board exams.

💻

Computer & Science Learning

Practical learning through the computer lab and science activities, so students connect classroom theory with the real world.

📖

Library & Reading

A school library and reading periods that build vocabulary, general knowledge and a lifelong habit of reading.

🏅

Tests & Assessment

Monthly tests, term examinations and parent–teacher meetings keep parents fully informed of every student's progress.

Morning Assembly & Discipline

Every day begins the right way

The school day at FG Public School starts on the green ground, with the whole school standing in neat rows for morning assembly — recitation, national anthem, and a short talk on good conduct.

  • Daily assembly with recitation and the national anthem
  • Full uniform check — white shirt, black trousers, black shoes
  • House and proctor system: senior boys lead by example
  • Punctuality, cleanliness and respect for teachers

Our proctors and prefects — easily recognised by their sashes — help maintain discipline and guide the junior students throughout the day.

Patriotism & National Days

Proud Pakistanis, from a young age

National days are celebrated with full spirit. Junior students hold the flags of Pakistan and Kashmir, wear thematic dress, and stand with the Principal and teachers to mark days like Independence Day and Kashmir Solidarity Day.

  • Flag-hoisting and national songs
  • Speeches, tableaus and thematic dress by junior classes
  • Love for Pakistan and respect for its defenders

Junior students with the Principal and teachers, holding the Pakistan and Kashmir flags on a national day.

Admissions 2026

How to join FG Public School

Admissions are announced by the school each year. For the 2026 session, the school's published schedule is:

🗓️ Admissions close: 31 March 2026  •  Classes commence: 06 April 2026Visit the School Office
1

Get the Form

Collect the admission form from the school office during school hours.

2

Attach Documents

Copy of B-Form / birth certificate, father's CNIC copy, previous result card and passport-size photographs.

3

Test / Interview

The student appears in the entry test / interview on the announced date, as applicable for his class.

4

Merit & Enrolment

Successful candidates complete fee and enrolment formalities before classes commence.

Who can apply?

Boys seeking admission from the junior classes up to Class IX (subject to seat availability), including children of serving and retired armed-forces personnel and civilians, as per FGEI admission policy and quota.

What is the school uniform?

White shirt, black trousers, black shoes and the school belt — worn neatly every day, as seen in our morning assembly photos. In winter, the prescribed sweater / blazer is added.

Where do I get exact dates and fee details?

Admission schedules and fee details are displayed on the school notice board each session. Please visit the school office in Kharian Cantt for the current information.
